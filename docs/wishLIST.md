## SupportOps Career Lab Wishlist — 30 Improvements

### Core learning upgrades

1. **Question-first modules**
   Every module starts with a scenario/question before teaching content.

2. **Confidence rating before answers**
   You rate confidence first, so the app can detect lucky guesses vs real knowledge.

3. **Explain-your-reasoning box**
   Especially for IT scenarios, because your thinking process matters more than memorising.

4. **Mixed assessment types**
   MCQ, short answer, order-the-steps, ticket note writing, scenario judgement.

5. **Feynman “explain it simply” mode**
   Forces you to explain concepts like DNS, DHCP, MFA, VLANs, Jamf, etc. in plain English.

6. **Concept sorting activities**
   Example: sort issues into “ICT owns”, “admin owns”, “escalate”, “needs more evidence”.

7. **Leitner flashcard boxes**
   Clear buckets like: New, Learning, Almost Known, Strong, Needs Review.

8. **Error log with “why it matters at DCS”**
   Not just “wrong answer”, but “this would affect a real classroom/printer/login issue”.

9. **Weak-area detector**
   Automatically shows your worst domains: passwords, printers, Wi-Fi, ViewBoards, tickets, M365, etc.

10. **Due Today review page**
    One place for flashcards, missed questions, weak scenarios, and note-writing practice.

---

### DCS-specific workflow upgrades

11. **DCS quiet-window dashboard**
    A homepage for quiet work pockets: “5-minute task”, “20-minute focus”, “review due cards”, “do one scenario”.

12. **Start Tiny mode**
    One tiny task only: one flashcard set, one question, one ticket-note drill.

13. **I’m Overwhelmed mode**
    Shows three simple choices: breathe, review one easy item, or write one tiny PD note.

14. **Realistic Scenario Lab**
    Teacher can’t display, printer queue stuck, parent portal issue, Sentral access-key problem, phishing email, Wi-Fi issue.

15. **Jira-style ticket note scoring**
    Scores who/where/device, symptom, scope, steps tried, urgency, privacy-safe wording, next action.

16. **Escalation quality trainer**
    Practise writing “what I checked, what I found, what I need next” without waffle.

17. **System ownership map**
    Clear visual guide: ICT, admin, leadership, external vendor, teacher-owned content, parent-facing systems.

18. **Role-boundary warnings**
    Built-in reminders: “This is awareness only, not Josh-owned admin action.”

19. **Privacy-safe wording trainer**
    Helps rewrite messy/internal notes into safe professional support language.

20. **DCS workflow decision trees**
    Example: “Parent can’t access portal” → login issue? access key? family details? admin handoff?

---

### Practical support-output upgrades

21. **Knowledge Base Lab**
    Turn repeated ticket themes into OurDCS-ready help articles.

22. **Practical output templates**
    Password reset guide, Wi-Fi checklist, ViewBoard quick fix, printer symptom matrix, onboarding checklist.

23. **Printable quick-reference cards**
    A4 or half-page sheets for common workflows: Windows + P, 169.254, Follow-Me printing, no audio.

24. **Room/device troubleshooting map**
    Track common classroom AV issues by room, without storing sensitive data.

25. **Ticket-theme tracker**
    Log recurring issue types: printers, ViewBoards, passwords, Parent Portal, Sentral, Wi-Fi.

26. **PD log with auto-summary**
    Logs what you studied, scenarios completed, outputs created, and weak areas improved.

27. **Evidence Pack export**
    Generates a manager-safe Markdown summary of your PD, growth, and practical outputs.

28. **Readiness graphs based on evidence**
    Not fake confidence charts — actual scoring from quizzes, scenarios, flashcards, outputs, and ticket-note quality.

---

### Bigger “make it brilliant” ideas

29. **AI tutor / coach mode**
    You answer a scenario, then the app gives feedback like: “Good first step, but you missed scope and escalation wording.”

30. **DCS + CompTIA A+ bridge mode**
    Shows how A+ concepts apply at DCS: DHCP → 169.254 laptop, HDMI → ViewBoard, printers → PaperCut, cloud → M365/Schoolbox.
